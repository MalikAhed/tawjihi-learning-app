The PDF itself is titled **العلوم الحياتية – العلمي والزراعي، الرزمة التعليمية 2024** (Gaza). Its contents page names the reduced curriculum and starts page numbers at 4. The four included broad units are:

* الوحدة الأولى: **تدفق الطاقة** — البناء الضوئي (التفاعلات الضوئية اللاحلقية/الحلقية، حلقة كالفن، العوامل المؤثرة) and التنفس الخلوي; **من الجين إلى البروتين** — الشيفرة الوراثية وبناء البروتين.
* الوحدة الثانية: **الوراثة** — قانون مندل، الصفات غير المندلية (السيادة غير التامة، السيادة المشتركة، الأليلات المتعددة, تحديد الجنس, المرتبطة/المتأثرة بالجنس, الارتباط والعبور, الاختلالات الوراثية). The PDF marks some entries with * للعلمي فقط.
* الوحدة الثالثة: أجهزة جسم الإنسان — **الجهاز الهيكلي** (أقسام، أشكال العظم، تركيب نسيج العظم), **جهاز الدوران** (تركيب ووظيفة), **الجهاز المناعي** (الأنظمة المناعية، المناعة الإيجابية والسلبية).
* الوحدة الثالثة also includes **البكتيريا** (تصنيف بدائيات النوى، الأشكال، تركيب الخلية) and **الفيروسات** (التركيب، الأشكال، التصنيف، التكاثر), with a worksheet and test.

The package's stated learning outcomes (page after contents) explicitly require: tracking cyclic/non-cyclic light reactions; Calvin-cycle products; environmental effects on photosynthesis; cellular-respiration stages; transcription/translation; Mendelian and non-Mendelian genetics; genetics–mathematics links; structure/function of skeletal, circulatory and immune systems; heartbeat/body defense; bacterial/viral properties and classification. This is a useful coverage checklist for a Gaza-specific set. ## Question-format evidence and recurring focus

The official Gaza 2024 package itself ends each major block with a worksheet and a test. Extracted page references (official PDF pages, printed page numbers in the package):

* Printed pp. 20–23 (energy flow + gene→protein test): MCQ on hydrogen's role, plant mass source, Krebs location, carbon count in oxaloacetate, proton-pump direction, codon count and hydrogen bonding; then multi-part numerical prompts converting Calvin-cycle ATP/G3P/NADPH/CO2/glucose and aerobic respiration FADH2 into glucose, direct ATP, NADH, CO2 and O2. The test also asks G3P/water photolysis, ATP stage identification, amino-acid codon degeneracy, DNA/mRNA/anticodon transcription, stage comparison, and Calvin-cycle calculations from fixed CO2.
* Printed pp. 37–41 (genetics worksheet/test): contrast incomplete vs codominance; blood antigens; sex-influenced vs sex-linked; linked-gene probability without crossing-over; map recombination; codon/anticodon and trisomy; blood type + color-blindness pedigree; linkage ratios such as 41.5/41.5/8.5/8.5 and explanation of 1:2:1 vs 9:3:3:1. This is direct evidence that Gaza's own test expects calculations, pedigrees, and multi-step written reasoning.
* Printed pp. 68–70 (human systems + microbes worksheet/test): MCQ on mobile vertebrae, hip joint, patella classification, forearm bones, tricuspid valve, T-cell maturation, bacterial arrangements, complex viruses, and DNA/RNA virus distinctions; short answers on red-cell adaptations, terms (capsid/interferon/spores), DNA-vs-RNA virus replication, immune-cell classes; then test items on foramen magnum, helper T regulation, atrial septal defect, oxygen-poor vessels, bacterial diseases/endospores, measles virus type, labeled heart figure, axial skeleton, bacterial/viral examples, Gram-positive/negative walls and phagocytosis steps.

The solved 1997–2020 dossier's first pages show repeated exam archetypes. Examples (preserved as summaries, not a republication):

* Mendelian crosses with pea traits, unknown parental genotype, phenotype ratios, Punnett squares, gamete sets and probabilities (1997; summer 2002; winter/summer 2003–2009; winter/summer 2011–2013). Typical asks: infer parental genotypes, predict a phenotype, compute a probability, identify test cross.
* MCQ genetics requiring distinction among incomplete dominance, codominance, multiple alleles, sex-linked/sex-influenced inheritance, lethal alleles, linkage and recombination; map distance is asked as a percentage.
* Molecular genetics: identify coding/template/anticodon sequence direction, count codons/amino acids, order translation events, identify ribosome sites (A/P/E), recognize restriction enzymes and gel electrophoresis, mutation types and recombinant-DNA applications.
* Energy flow: quantify ATP/NADH/NADPH/CO2/O2 in photosynthesis, Calvin cycle, glycolysis/Krebs/electron transport and fermentation; locate each stage by organelle; reason from environmental-factor graphs (light intensity, temperature, bicarbonate/CO2) and identify saturation points.
* Human systems and microorganisms: classify bones/axial vs appendicular, blood groups and antigen/antibody test cards, circulation/heart, immune response and active/passive immunity, bacterial shape/structure/classification, virus structure/classification/replication.
* Figures/tables are integral: Punnett squares, genetic maps, blood-typing cards, photosynthesis gas-bubble graph, Calvin-cycle diagrams, translation diagrams and structural drawings. Generated questions should include image/diagram interpretation, not only text recall.

## Use and reliability notes

* The Gaza package is the best curriculum boundary for the user's request; include only its listed chapters unless a later Ministry notice proves a change. The broader West Bank textbook and 1997–2020 dossier include extra gene-technology material, so tag those questions **outside Gaza-2024 reduced scope** when they are absent from the Gaza package.
* Search result sites are mirrors; direct files may be large or transient. Save source URL + retrieval date and inspect every answer against the official paper/textbook. Do not claim “guaranteed >90%”; a comprehensive set can improve coverage but cannot guarantee a grade.

## 2026 Gaza format update

The Ministry's 20 June 2026 launch notice confirms that Gaza's examinations were delivered electronically for the third consecutive year: <https://www.moe.edu.ps/c/30744>. A contemporary report quoting the Ministry's Gaza notice describes ordinary subjects as 40 four-choice, one-correct-answer items in 60 minutes, delivered through Wise School where connectivity permits with a paper fallback: <https://felesteen.news/post/181819/>. No separately indexed 2026 Biology deletion list was found; the 2024 Gaza reduced package remains the curriculum boundary.
