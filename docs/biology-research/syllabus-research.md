The mirror's indexed table of contents explicitly marks the starred chapters as **للعلمي فقط** and gives the page starts above (lines 96–124). ## What the pack expects students to do (high-value learning outcomes)

- Trace cyclic/non-cyclic light reactions and Calvin-cycle outputs; infer how environmental factors affect photosynthetic rate; trace cellular respiration (indexed lines 146–150).
- Solve Mendelian crosses, including two or more traits, gamete counting and branching method (indexed lines 664–706).
- Explain and calculate non-Mendelian cases: incomplete dominance, codominance, ABO/multiple alleles, sex systems, sex-linked/sex-influenced traits, linkage/crossing-over and mutation patterns (indexed lines 717–724).
- Explain structure–function relations and mechanisms, not just definitions: RBC adaptation, heart flow/valves, innate/acquired immunity and phagocytosis, bacterial structures, and virus replication.

## Exam/worksheet style evidence from the pack itself

The pack contains a worksheet on pp. 68–69 and a test on pp. 69–70. Indexed examples (<https://www.scribd.com/document/1024476850/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%88%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%A1%D9%A2-%D9%85%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B2%D8%AC%D8%A9-%D8%BA%D8%B2%D8%A9>, lines 1540–1623) show the likely question mix:

- MCQ recognition/application: number of movable vertebrae; bone articulating with acetabulum; patella classification; forearm bones; tricuspid valve; T-cell maturation; bacterial arrangements; complex virus; DNA/RNA disease distinction.
- Short constructed response: RBC structure/function; define sex pili, endospores, capsid, interferon; compare DNA- and RNA-virus replication; compare NK/B/T cells by proportion and immune system.
- Diagram/clinical application: atrial septal defect; oxygen-poor vessel; heart chamber/valve diagram; classify blood; explain wall differences in Gram-positive/negative bacteria; sequence phagocytosis.
- The test uses the same styles and cross-unit integration (e.g., spinal canal/foramen magnum, helper/suppressor T cells, atrial septum, pulmonary artery, bacterial spores, measles virus shape/genome, diagram labels and “what if a valve is absent?”).

**Implication for the app bank:** each retained topic needs MCQ distractors, a short explanation/flashcard, and at least one “why/what happens if/compare/diagram” application. A purely definition-based bank would miss the pack's own assessed formats.

## Current uncertainty / version control

- I found no authoritative, text-searchable MoE document that enumerates a separate 2026 deletion list for Biology. The official portal currently serves the Gaza pack and is copyright-marked 2026, but the indexed Biology pack itself is labelled 2024. Do not claim that the 2024 pack equals every 2025/2026 cohort until the ministry posts an explicit update.
- The same ministry portal has a current Grade 12 scientific course index at <https://elearn.edu.ps/c12s/>. Its Biology section lists, in 2026, photosynthesis (including Calvin-cycle and quantitative applications), cellular respiration and fermentation, gene-to-protein, Mendelian and non-Mendelian genetics (linkage/maps, disorders, incomplete/common dominance, multiple alleles, lethal genes, sex-linked/sex-influenced traits), applications of genetics, the skeletal/circulatory/immune systems, and viruses (indexed lines 572–659). The portal's 2026 review page <https://elearn.edu.ps/category/tvprog/rev12bio2026/> lists dedicated review lessons for energy, photosynthesis, cellular respiration and gene-to-protein dated 3 May 2026 (lines 86–120). These are strong evidence of what the ministry is actively teaching in 2026, but they are the regular Grade 12 scientific stream and do not by themselves prove a Gaza exam's reduced requirements.
- The Wafa notice establishes that inside-Gaza students use the packs; outside-Gaza students use the regular 2025 curriculum. Ask the product owner/student whether they are inside Gaza and which cohort (2024/2025/2026) before locking “required” coverage.
- The Scribd copy is a mirror and carries a copyright warning. Do not copy its full text/questions wholesale. It is safe to use the page map, topics, learning outcomes, and question-style observations as research; author original Arabic questions and cite the official pack link.

## Full-book comparison (why the Gaza pack is reduced)

The ministry's full Grade 12 scientific/agricultural Biology book is available at <https://elearn.azureedge.net/Books/c12/12s/sc12s.pdf> (180 PDF pages; cover says third edition, 2020). Its contents page is indexed at PDF page 4. Comparing that authoritative contents page (indexed lines 93–237 of <https://elearn.azureedge.net/Books/c12/12s/sc12s.pdf>) with the 70-page Gaza pack gives a practical exclusion map:

| Full-book material present in the 180-page book | Evidence in full-book TOC | Not present as a named section in the 2024 Gaza pack |
|---|---|---|
| **Bacteria**: historical overview (for reading), habitats, genetic-material transfer, growth/reproduction, growth conditions, growth control, antibiotics, importance | lines 93–104 | Gaza keeps only prokaryote classification, forms, and bacterial-cell structure (pp. 58–64). |
| **Viruses**: historical overview (for reading), diseases, viral biotechnology | lines 105–112 | Gaza keeps structure, forms, classification and replication (pp. 65–67). |
| **Non-Mendelian/Genetics**: “genetic traits” section, genetic maps, and the full applications-of-genetics chapter (genetic engineering, tools, recombinant DNA, electrophoresis/fingerprint, applications, ethics) | lines 161–179 | Gaza keeps inheritance patterns through linkage/crossing-over and genetic abnormalities (pp. 27–41), with no named genetic-maps or gene-technology chapter. DNA fingerprint/engineering questions in regular national papers therefore need an out-of-scope tag unless the student's cohort notice explicitly reinstates them. |
| **Skeletal system**: functions, bone formation/growth, cartilage, joints, skeletal disorders | lines 197–206 | Gaza keeps parts, bone shapes and bone-tissue structure only (pp. 42–47). |
| **Circulatory system**: blood clotting/wound healing and circulatory diseases | lines 207–210 | Gaza keeps heart/circulation structure and blood components only (pp. 48–52). |
| **Immune system**: antibody structure/functions, immune disorders, organ donation (organ donation marked for reading) | lines 211–217 | Gaza keeps immune systems and active/passive immunity only (pp. 53–57). |

This comparison is the strongest available evidence for what was removed from the Gaza educational pack. It is based on named section headings, so a teacher's local instruction could still assign a small cross-reference; keep excluded sections hidden/flagged until the student's official cohort guidance confirms them.
